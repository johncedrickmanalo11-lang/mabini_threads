Hi! I'm John Cedrick Manalo, the creator of Mabini Threads. This website combines my interest in web development, e-commerce, design, and the beautiful places of my hometown, Mabini, Batangas.

Mabini Threads is a personal portfolio project that connects a clothing e-commerce platform with a tourism information site — every product is named after a real place in town.
